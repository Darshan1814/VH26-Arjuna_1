ERROR CODE E101: Spindle Motor Overheating Detected.
SYMPTOM: Motor housing temperature exceeds 85°C. System stops with thermal fault alarm.
PROBABLE CAUSES:
1. Spindle cooling fan failure or disconnected power cable
2. Air intake ventilation filters clogged with chips and coolant mist
3. Heavy continuous cut overload exceeding 120% duty cycle rating

CORRECTIVE ACTION:
1. Stop the CNC spindle immediately and allow 20 minutes cooling period.
2. Inspect the 24V DC auxiliary cooling fan behind the spindle headstock.
3. Clean or replace the metal mesh air ventilation filters.
4. Check spindle motor load meter on the CNC operator panel.
