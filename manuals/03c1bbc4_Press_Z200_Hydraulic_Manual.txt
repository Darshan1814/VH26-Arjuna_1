ERROR CODE E101: Hydraulic Circuit Pressure Low.
SYMPTOM: Main system manifold pressure drops below 140 bar during ram descent.
PROBABLE CAUSES:
1. Hydraulic fluid level in main 500L reservoir below minimum sight glass
2. Proportional pressure relief valve solenoid sticking or burned out
3. Main variable displacement hydraulic pump suction strainer clogged

CORRECTIVE ACTION:
1. Emergency stop hydraulic power unit (HPU) immediately.
2. Inspect hydraulic fluid reservoir level and top off with ISO VG 46 oil.
3. Check proportional relief valve coil resistance (nominal 24 Ohms).
4. Service suction line strainer and bleed trapped air from manifold block.
